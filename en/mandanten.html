What you need to know now

Is my case right for you?

If you were involved in a traffic accident through no fault of your own, you have come to the right place. Traffic law has been our sole focus for 16 years – we represent injured parties exclusively, never the insurer.

What does this cost me?

After a no-fault accident, the other party's liability insurer as a rule bears the necessary legal fees. You do not need legal expenses insurance for this. We tell you clearly what applies in your case before you instruct us.

What should I do now – and what should I definitely not do?

Do not sign anything that the other party's insurer puts in front of you, and do not let an appraiser be pushed on you – you have the right to your own independent expert. You will find the most important immediate steps in the checklist further down.

Will I receive more than the insurer offers?

Often yes. Diminished value, loss of use, residual value, rental car and compensation for pain and suffering are readily overlooked by insurers. We quantify every item – not the insurer.

How much effort does this involve for me?

Little. You describe the accident to us once – by WhatsApp, photo or email. We take over all communication with the insurer, the appraiser and the authorities.

How quickly can I reach you?

As a rule within 24 hours – directly with the attorney, personally reachable. In German, Turkish or English.

Can I trust you?

Immediate checklist

The most important steps at the scene of the accident

  • 1Secure the accident site. Hazard lights on, set up the warning triangle, wear a high-visibility vest.
  • 2Attend to anyone injured. In the event of personal injury, call the emergency services immediately on 112.
  • 3Call the police (110). Especially in the event of major damage, a dispute or personal injury.
  • 4Document everything. Photos of the vehicles, the damage, the registration numbers, the final positions and the surroundings.
  • 5Exchange details. Name, address, registration number, insurer – note down any witnesses.
  • 6Sign nothing. Do not admit fault – speak to us first.
  • 7Contact us. We take over from here and pursue your claims.

Good to know

After a no-fault accident you have the right to an appraiser of your choice and a lawyer of your choice – the costs are as a rule borne by the other party's insurer.

Your questions – in order

What you need to know now

Is my case right for you?

If you were involved in a traffic accident through no fault of your own, you are in exactly the right place. Traffic law has been our sole focus for 16 years – we represent injured parties only, never the insurer.

What will it cost me?

After a non-fault accident, the necessary legal fees are usually borne by the other party's liability insurer. You do not need legal expenses insurance for this. We tell you clearly what applies in your case before you engage us.

What should I do now – and what must I never do?

Do not sign anything the other party's insurer puts in front of you, and don't let an assessor be forced on you – you have the right to your own independent assessor. The most important immediate steps are in the checklist below.

Will I get more than the insurer offers?

Often yes. Loss of value, loss of use, residual value, rental car and pain-and-suffering are frequently overlooked by insurers. We quantify every item – not the insurer.

How much effort is this for me?

Little. You describe the accident to us once – via WhatsApp, photo or e-mail. We handle all communication with the insurer, the assessor and the authorities.

How quickly can I reach you?

Usually within 24 hours – directly with the attorney. In German, Turkish or English.

Can I trust you?

Immediate checklist

The most important steps at the accident scene

  • 1Secure the accident scene. Hazard lights on, set up the warning triangle, wear a safety vest.
  • 2Attend to the injured. In case of injury, immediately call 112.
  • 3Call the police (110). Especially for major damage, disputes or personal injury.
  • 4Document everything. Photos of the vehicles, damage, licence plates, final positions and surroundings.
  • 5Exchange details. Names, addresses, licence plates, insurance – note down witnesses.
  • 6Do not sign anything. Do not admit fault – speak to us first.
  • 7Contact us. We take over from here and pursue your claims.

Good to know

After a non-fault accident you are entitled to an independent assessor you trust and a lawyer you trust – the costs are usually borne by the opposing insurer.

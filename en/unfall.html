Your legal right

What you are entitled to after the accident

After a no-fault accident you do not have to accept either the appraiser or the repair shop of the other party's insurer: you have the right to your own independent expert – whose costs are as a rule borne by the other side. Items such as diminished value, loss of use or loss of earnings are, as a rule, part of the recoverable damage as well. We quantify each one and assert it.

We know the other side

Typical strategies of the insurer – and how we counter them

Quick offer, quick signature

“Uncomplicated claims management” serves above all to keep costs down. We examine all claims before anything is signed.

The insurer's appraiser

Experience shows that the insurer's appraisal report often turns out lower. You have the right to your own independent expert – the costs are as a rule borne by the other side.

Diminished value overlooked

A repaired accident vehicle is as a rule worth less when sold. Without a lawyer this item is often overlooked – we quantify it and assert it.

Loss of use & rental car reduced

For the duration of the repair or replacement purchase, a rental car or loss of use may come into consideration – the amount depends on the vehicle class. We examine the applicable classification.

Notional settlement calculated down

Those who settle on the basis of the appraisal report readily see items struck out. Whether a reduction holds depends on the individual case – we examine every one.

Delaying & stalling

No response, in order to gain time. We set deadlines and pursue the claim consistently – up to court proceedings if necessary.

Your questions before we start

What does instructing you cost me?

After a no-fault traffic accident, the other party's liability insurer regularly bears the necessary legal fees. Legal expenses insurance is not required for this. In all other constellations we clarify the question of costs transparently before an engagement begins.

I do not have legal expenses insurance.

After a no-fault accident you do not need any: the necessary legal fees are as a rule borne by the other party's liability insurer.

I am not sure whether I am at fault.

Describe the accident to us – we examine the liability situation and tell you clearly how your prospects stand before you commit yourself.

Do I have to accept the insurer's appraiser?

No. After a no-fault accident you may instruct your own independent expert – whose costs are as a rule borne by the other party's insurer. Experience shows that the insurer's appraisal report often turns out lower.

The insurer is offering a quick, uncomplicated settlement – should I accept?

Be careful: quick offers and the “claims management” of the other party's insurer serve above all to keep costs down. Do not sign a settlement release before all claims have been examined.

How long does the settlement take?

Simple bodywork damage is often settled within 4–6 weeks. Where there are personal injuries or liability is disputed, it can take longer. We set deadlines and follow them up.

The damage is small – is a lawyer worthwhile?

Often yes. Small claims in particular contain overlooked items such as diminished value, loss of use or appraiser's fees, which we assert for you.

Immediately after the accident

The five most important steps at the scene of the accident

  1. Sign nothing – no admission of fault, no quick settlement from the insurer.
  2. Take photos – vehicles, damage, registration numbers, surroundings.
  3. Note down witnesses – name and telephone number are enough.
  4. Do not take calls from the other party's insurer – anyone offering to “settle everything without complications” represents the other side. Your own insurer, by contrast, should be notified of the accident within the applicable deadline.
  5. Contact us – we take care of everything further from here.

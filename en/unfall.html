Your three most important questions

What will it cost me?

After a non-fault accident, the other party's liability insurer usually covers the legal fees – you don't need legal expenses insurance for this.

Must I use the insurer's assessor?

No. You have the right to your own independent assessor – the costs are usually borne by the other side.

How fast is it?

You usually receive a clear initial assessment within 24 hours. After that we handle the entire settlement.

Or send a written enquiry right away

The 5 most important steps after an accident

  1. Do not sign anything – no admission of fault, no quick settlement offers.
  2. Take photos – vehicles, damage, licence plates, surroundings.
  3. Note witnesses – name and phone number are enough.
  4. Do not take calls from the opposing insurer – whoever offers to “sort it all out easily” is acting for the other side. Do report the accident to your own insurer within the applicable deadline.
  5. Contact us – we take over from here.

Common concerns – cleared up

I don't have legal expenses insurance.

After a non-fault accident you don't need it: the necessary legal fees are usually borne by the other party's liability insurer.

I'm not sure whether I'm at fault.

Describe the accident to us – we assess the liability situation and tell you clearly what your chances are before you commit.

I don't live in Wiesbaden.

No problem. We work nationwide and digitally – the entire process runs via WhatsApp, e-mail and phone.

The damage is small – is a lawyer worth it?

Often yes. Small damage in particular contains overlooked items such as loss of value, loss of use or assessor fees, which we assert on your behalf.

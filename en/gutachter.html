What the Federal Court of Justice decided in 2024

In 2024 the German Federal Court of Justice (BGH) clarified (among others, judgment of 12 March 2024 – VI ZR 280/22): even invoice items of the expert that are excessive without this being apparent to the injured party remain recoverable from the other party's insurer. Blanket fee reductions therefore frequently come to nothing – we take consistent action against blanket reductions.

We know the other side

Typical reduction strategies – and how we counter them

Basic fee reduced

Reference to the insurer's own “fee tables”. Under the 2024 case law of the Federal Court of Justice (among others VI ZR 280/22), the risk of the level of the fee is borne by the party causing the damage – we challenge the reduction of the basic fee.

Ancillary costs struck out

Travel, photo and writing costs are reduced across the board. Items that are not apparently excessive remain recoverable from the insurer.

Review reports from external service providers

“Review appraisals” are meant to calculate the invoice down. We know the arguments of these service providers – and how to meet them.

Residual value & diminished value

Disputed amounts are calculated downwards. We quantify every item properly – not the insurer.

The expert's fee risk

The argument that the injured party should have instructed someone “cheaper” frequently comes to nothing under current case law.

Delay & stalling

No response, in order to gain time. We set deadlines and pursue the claim consistently – up to court proceedings if necessary.

Your questions before we start

Does the law office specialise in working with experts?

Yes. Traffic law has been our focus for 16 years, and asserting appraisers' fees is part of our core business. We know the arguments of the insurers and their review service providers.

How do you proceed against reduced appraisers' fees?

We rely on the 2024 case law of the Federal Court of Justice on the expert's fee risk (among others VI ZR 280/22): the risk of the level of the fee is borne by the party causing the damage. We do not accept blanket reductions of the basic fee and of ancillary costs.

Will work start immediately – or will my appraisal report simply sit there?

You receive our assessment as a rule within 24 hours. After that you never have to chase us – at every procedural step (letter of claim, the insurer's reply, payment, court action where necessary) we get in touch unprompted.

Does the law office understand the typical reductions (ancillary costs, review reports)?

Yes. Reduced travel, photo and writing costs, references to fee tables or review reports from service providers are familiar to us from daily practice – and we know how to meet them.

Is my neutrality as an expert preserved?

Yes. The choice of appraiser always rests with the injured party; we make no promises about referring instructions or clients – not least for professional-conduct reasons. Your independence is the basis for the evidential value of your appraisal report.

Who bears the costs of asserting the claim through a lawyer?

Our fees are governed by the German Lawyers' Remuneration Act (RVG). After a no-fault accident, the necessary costs of pursuing the claim are as a rule part of the recoverable damage and are regularly borne by the other party's liability insurer. In all other constellations we clarify the question of costs transparently before an engagement begins.

Am I bound by any exclusivity?

No. There is no exclusivity agreement and no minimum volumes. You work with us when that is the best solution for your case – case by case or on an ongoing basis.

How does the handover work – and who do I work with?

By WhatsApp or email: the appraisal report, the invoice and – with their consent – the contact details of the injured party. You always work directly with attorney Serpil Şahin, one permanent point of contact, in German, Turkish or English.

You never have to chase us – we get in touch first: at every procedural step you receive a message unprompted. You know at all times where your money stands.

Also for vehicle repair shops

Repair invoice reduced? The same approach

Repair shops are hit in similar places to experts, only with different items. Typical are reduced hourly labour rates with reference to an independent garage, struck-out transfer costs, deducted parts price surcharges (UPE) and the assertion that the repair time charged is too long. Whether any of these reductions holds depends on the individual case – in particular on whether the vehicle is actually repaired or settled notionally, and on whether the alternative garage named is equivalent and reasonable.

The route to us is the same: you hand us the invoice together with the reduction letter, we examine it and represent you vis-à-vis the insurer. Simply use the same route for this.

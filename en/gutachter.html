Cooperation with accident assessors

Are you a vehicle assessor looking for a law firm active in traffic law as a reliable partner for claims enforcement? Get in touch.

⚖ BGH 2024

The assessor risk lies with the party at fault – not with you.

Germany's Federal Court of Justice clarified in 2024 (judgment of 12 March 2024 – VI ZR 280/22): even invoice items of the assessor that are excessive without the injured party being able to recognise it remain recoverable from the opposing insurer. Blanket fee cuts therefore frequently fail – we enforce assessor invoices consistently.

We know the other side

Typical insurer cut strategies – and how we counter them

Base fee cut

Reference to their own „fee tables". Under the 2024 Federal Court ruling (VI ZR 280/22) the party at fault bears the fee-level risk – we enforce the base fee.

Ancillary costs deleted

Travel, photo and writing fees are cut flat. Items not obviously excessive remain recoverable from the insurer.

Assessors' review reports

„Review reports" try to shrink the invoice. We know these firms' arguments – and how to counter them.

Salvage & diminished value

Disputed amounts are calculated down. We quantify every item properly – not the insurer.

Expert risk

The argument that the injured party should have chosen „cheaper" often fails under current case law.

Delay & stalling

Your questions before you start

Does the firm specialise in cooperation with experts?

Yes. Traffic law has been our focus for 16 years, and enforcing expert fees is core business. We know the arguments of insurers and their audit service providers.

Are reduced expert fees enforced consistently?

Yes. We rely on the BGH's 2024 case law on the expert risk (including VI ZR 280/22): the risk of the fee amount lies with the party at fault. We do not accept flat cuts to the base fee or ancillary costs.

Do you act immediately – or does my report sit around?

You usually receive our assessment within 24 hours. After that you never have to chase us – at every step (demand letter, insurer's response, payment, court if needed) we contact you unprompted.

Does the firm understand the typical cuts (ancillary costs, audit reports)?

Yes. Reduced travel, photo and writing costs, references to fee tables or audit reports from service providers are familiar to us from daily practice – and we know how to counter them.

Is my neutrality as an expert preserved?

Yes. The choice of expert always lies with the injured party; we make no promises about referring assignments or clients – for professional-law reasons. Your independence is the basis of your report's evidentiary value.

Who bears the costs of legal enforcement?

Fees are governed by the German Attorneys' Remuneration Act (RVG). After a no-fault accident, the necessary enforcement costs are generally part of the recoverable damages and are usually borne by the other party's insurer. In all other situations we clarify the cost question transparently before an engagement begins.

Am I bound by exclusivity?

No. There is no exclusivity agreement and no minimum volume. You work with us when it is the best solution for your case – case by case or on an ongoing basis.

How does handover work – and who do I work with?

Via WhatsApp or e-mail: the report, the invoice and – with their consent – the injured party's contact details. You always work directly with attorney Serpil Şahin, one fixed contact, in German, Turkish or English.

You never have to chase us – we contact you first: at every step you receive an unprompted update. You always know where your money stands.

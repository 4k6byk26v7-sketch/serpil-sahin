Fill in the power of attorney – right here, in three steps

You fill in the power of attorney on your phone, tablet or computer, sign with your finger or your mouse and send the finished PDF directly to the law office. Every field explains what is meant.

Your details stay on your device. The PDF is created directly in your browser – nothing is transmitted to a server. Only when you tap “Send” do you send the file yourself.

Step 1 of 3

Who is granting the power of attorney?

The person instructing the attorney – exactly as written in your ID document. Example: “Yılmaz, Ahmet”.

Your home address. Example: “Musterstraße 12, 65189 Wiesbaden”.

Step 2 of 3

Which case is it about?

The day of the accident. If you do not know it exactly, leave the field empty – we will clarify it together.

The registration number of your vehicle. Example: “WI-AB 123”. You may leave this field empty as well.

Step 3 of 3

Place, date and signature

The place where you are signing right now. Today's date is inserted automatically.

Sign here with your finger or your mouse

Sign as you would sign on paper.

Your power of attorney is ready.

Check the PDF briefly – and then send it directly to the law office.

View / download PDF

“Send” opens the share menu of your device – there you choose WhatsApp or your email programme. If your device does not offer this: download the PDF and attach it in WhatsApp (+49 162 62 100 87) or by email to info@serpil-sahin.de.

Prefer paper?

Here is the blank power of attorney as a PDF – print it, fill it in, sign it and send a photo of it by WhatsApp or email. We are happy to answer any questions in advance.

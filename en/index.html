Your claim

What you are entitled to after a non-fault accident

Eight items that regularly come together – we quantify each one and assert it.

  • Repair or replacementRepair costs on the basis of an assessor report, or the replacement value in case of a total loss.
  • Diminished valueA repaired accident vehicle is worth less on resale. Without a lawyer this item is often overlooked.
  • Loss of use or rental carFor the duration of repair or replacement – usually 23 to 175 euros per day depending on vehicle class.
  • Compensation for pain and sufferingIn case of injury. First offers from insurers are typically well below what is enforceable.
  • An assessor you trustYou do not have to accept the other side's assessor. The cost is usually borne by the opposing insurer.
  • Loss of earningsIn case of accident-related incapacity to work – including inability to run your household.
  • Incidental costsTowing, de-registration and registration, travel to medical appointments.
  • Legal feesAfter a non-fault accident these are usually borne by the opposing liability insurer.

Frequently asked questions

Answers after a traffic accident

What does instructing you cost me?

In a non-fault traffic accident, the other party's liability insurer regularly covers the necessary legal fees. No legal expenses insurance is required for this.

Do I need legal expenses insurance?

No. In a non-fault accident the other party's insurer usually covers the costs. If you are at fault, legal expenses insurance helps – we clarify this with you in advance.

May I choose an assessor I trust?

Yes. In a non-fault accident you have the right to your own independent assessor – not the one from the other party's insurer.

Do you also represent nationwide?

Yes. The process is digital, so we can represent you throughout Germany.

The insurer offers a quick, simple settlement – should I accept?

Caution: quick offers and "claims management" by the opposing insurer mainly serve to cut costs. Do not sign a settlement release before all claims have been reviewed.
